Return Guide

What can be returned?

These general guidelines help determine whether an item may qualify.

Size Doesn't Fit

If your footwear doesn't fit as expected, an exchange may be requested subject to size availability and applicable conditions.

Damaged on Arrival

If your order arrives damaged, contact support promptly with photographs of the product and packaging.

Wrong Item

If you received a different product from what you ordered, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Used Footwear

Items that show signs of outdoor wear, damage, washing or alteration may not qualify for return or exchange.
