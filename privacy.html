A clear and simple approach to privacy.

DESISTEPS aims to collect information relevant to operating the website, supporting customers and providing requested services.

01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how DESISTEPS may handle information provided when you browse our website, contact us, place an order, request support or otherwise interact with our online services.

By using the website, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described in this policy, subject to applicable law and the terms that apply to the relevant service.

Important: This page is a project-ready privacy policy template. Confirm your actual legal entity, business contact details, payment providers, analytics services, cookie technologies and data practices before publishing it as a final legal document.
02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

Contact Details

Information such as your name, email address, phone number or other details you choose to provide when contacting us.

Order Details

Information needed to process and support an order, such as selected products, delivery information and order references.

Messages

Information contained in support requests, contact forms, WhatsApp conversations or other communications you initiate.

Technical Data

Depending on the technologies enabled on the website, technical information such as browser, device or basic website interaction data may be processed.

We do not intend to collect sensitive personal information through ordinary website forms unless a specific service requires it and appropriate safeguards and notices are provided.

03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries, support requests and messages.
  • Processing and managing orders where online ordering is enabled.
  • Communicating with you about an order, request or service.
  • Providing information you have requested from DESISTEPS.
  • Maintaining, operating and improving the website.
  • Detecting, preventing or investigating misuse and security issues.
  • Meeting applicable legal, accounting or administrative requirements.

We should not use personal information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate legal basis or notice where required.

04

Sharing Information

DESISTEPS may need to share relevant information with service providers that help operate the website or fulfill a request.

  • Payment or transaction providers, where payments are enabled.
  • Shipping or delivery partners, where an order requires delivery.
  • Website hosting, security, analytics or technical service providers.
  • Professional or legal advisers where reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Service providers should receive only information reasonably required for the relevant service, subject to the arrangements and legal requirements applicable to the relationship.

05

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, support essential functions, understand website usage or improve the user experience.

The specific cookies used by DESISTEPS will depend on the tools and services actually installed on the website.

Before launch: If the website uses Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising tools, payment widgets, embedded media or other third-party technologies, update the privacy and cookie notices accordingly.
06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs, or where retention is required by applicable law.

Actual retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the reason it was collected and relevant legal or operational requirements.

08

Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

Access Request access to relevant personal information.
Correction Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
Deletion Request deletion where applicable.
Restriction Request limits on certain processing where available.
Portability Request data portability where applicable.
Contact Ask questions about how information is handled.

These rights are not necessarily absolute and may be subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

09

Children's Privacy

The website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is restricted by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, contact DESISTEPS so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action considered.

10

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technologies, legal requirements or business practices.

When the policy changes, the updated version should be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date where appropriate.

11

Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, request or concern, contact DESISTEPS using the support details published on the website.

Questions about your privacy?

Our support team is here to help with privacy-related questions.
