GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things To Remember.

Helpful information before your DesiSteps journey begins.

Processing

Orders generally need time to be processed and packed before dispatch. Processing times may vary during sales, holidays and peak periods.

Delivery Days

Delivery estimates are approximate. Weather, public holidays, courier delays and serviceability can affect arrival times.

Address

Please make sure your shipping address, phone number and PIN code are correct before completing your order.

SHIPPING FAQ

Questions? We've Got You.

Here are some common shipping questions from our community.

Delivery times depend on your location and courier service. As a general guide, domestic orders may arrive within several business days after dispatch. The actual delivery estimate shown for your order should be treated as the most relevant one.

When tracking is available for your order, the tracking information can be shared after your package has been dispatched.

Contact us as soon as possible if you notice an address mistake. Address changes may not be possible once an order has been dispatched.

Delivery timelines can occasionally change because of courier operations, weather, holidays or other unforeseen circumstances. If your package appears delayed, contact our support team with your order details.

Serviceability depends on the courier network and destination PIN code. You can contact us before ordering if you would like help checking your delivery location.
