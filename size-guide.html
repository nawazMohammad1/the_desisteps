Fit Notes

Little things matter.

Use these simple checks before choosing your pair.

Measure both feet

One foot can be slightly longer than the other. Use the longer measurement.

Measure later in the day

Feet can naturally expand during the day, so a later measurement can be useful.

Think about how you'll wear them

Consider socks for closed shoes and the intended styling of the pair.

Check the product page

Juttis, Kolhapuris, sneakers and other styles can have different fit characteristics.

Need Help?

Fit questions, answered.

If your measurement sits between sizes, check the specific product guidance. The best choice can depend on the footwear construction and the fit you prefer.

If you normally wear the footwear with socks, measuring with the type of socks you expect to use can give a more relevant result.

Measure both feet and use the longer foot when comparing against the chart.

Use the charts as a general reference. Individual footwear styles can fit differently, so product-specific sizing information should take priority.
